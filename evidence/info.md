# Verification evidence

`m1-campus-map/` contains the preserved source identification screenshot and the
production preview's desktop/mobile, selected-building, failure-path and measured
browser results. See its folder guide and milestones/milestone.md for acceptance
and limitations. Screenshots show supplied geometry, not a claim of surveyed
architectural fidelity.

`m2-campus-refinement/` records expanded coverage and navigation checks, Paulson
facade/massing views, and credited architect reference images used for research.
References are not application textures. M1 screenshots remain historical evidence.

m3-campus-details/ contains source-photo references, final landmark screenshots, browser results and three color-study renders plus mobile evidence.

m4-campus-details/ holds Kimmel/Silver Center photographic references, multi-side renders, planting views, independent source restoration audit and browser results.

m5-window-details/ contains frozen-camera before/after Courant/Stern captures, source buffer/color invariants and browser acceptance results.

m6-streets-parks/ contains offline source provenance/calibration checks and before/after overview/close views of streets and parks, plus browser acceptance.

m7-road-depth/ records moving-camera zoom/pan checks and before/after asphalt evidence.
