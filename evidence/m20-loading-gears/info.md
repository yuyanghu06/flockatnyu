Reference: https://techatnyu.vercel.app/#teams. reference-layout.json and
reference-desktop.png record its 1440px layout; reference-geometry.json records
exact gear path hashes, radii, tooth counts, centers and projection.

state-results.json: actual state gate and fake-DOM/static tests.
motion-results.json: reference vectors, ratios, wrap and baked-keyframe checks.
browser-results.json: direct installed Chrome results and viewport measurements.
compositor-layers.json: actual Chrome layer tree and acceleration reasons for
three rotors and five phrases. loading/ready-desktop and mobile captures show the
final original-size gears shifted left with one CTA on the right. entered-desktop
shows actual map entry; failed-desktop shows the same CTA offering retry.

first-pass/ preserves the superseded SVG frame-loop implementation and title
column. Final evidence does not claim physical touch tests, full startup CPU
elimination, independent specialists or the original user's Chrome tab.
