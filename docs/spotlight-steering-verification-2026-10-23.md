# Spotlight controls and active reply input — October 23, 2026

Clear is black. The composer shows the former animated pastel square while a
reply runs; the separate header Stop is removed. A faint gray arrow appears
beside each queued message's X. Clicking it adds supported text to the active
conversation. An operation already executing finishes and keeps its receipt;
unstarted old proposals are skipped. A streaming answer draft resets and the
next answer uses the added text.

HTTP acceptance alone does not remove a row. The reply stream must confirm that
the text entered the conversation. Failed, closed or unconfirmed injections stay
queued and pause automatic dispatch. Supported inputs are text without files,
with the same building captured by the active message. Disabled arrows explain
why other entries need a separate reply. The square still stops the current
reply and pauses the queue; Enter continues to queue drafts during a reply.

## Boundaries and release

The inbox is bounded and process-local, protected by a random per-stream
capability, fresh authentication, exact Origin and unchanged principal/source
scope. Tokens never enter prompts, history or audit storage. Restarted/closed or
misrouted runs reject rather than silently starting another run. Completed
receipts, native call IDs, create identities, total budgets and deadline survive
corrections. Existing-target updates may represent a new human action; creates
and uncertain-write guards retain run-wide replay protection. No callable tool
performs another service's operation. Discord behavior, model/provider settings,
limits, schemas and worker deployments are unchanged.

Hosted deployment and read-only checks are pending. Browser transport simulation
and model checks on simulated storage do not establish a live business-data edit.
